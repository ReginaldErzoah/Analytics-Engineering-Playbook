# YouTube Channels For Analytics Engineering, Data Engineering, And Modern Data Platforms

## Overview

YouTube is one of the best resources for learning modern data engineering because many concepts are demonstrated through practical examples.

A strong learning strategy is to combine:

- Official channels
- Industry experts
- Project-based tutorials
- Conference talks

This list is organized by learning area.
