I will structure this as a **practical resource map**, not just a random list of links. The goal is to help you become job-ready for the roles you are targeting:

* Data Analyst
* BI Analyst
* Analytics Engineer
* Data Engineer
* Data Scientist
* ML Engineer (long term)

I will prioritize **high-quality resources used by professionals**, not beginner-only tutorials.
