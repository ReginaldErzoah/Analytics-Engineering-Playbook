# Career Growth Checklist For Analytics Engineers

## Overview

Career growth requires intentional progress across:

```
Technical Skills

+

Professional Evidence

+

Visibility

+

Career Strategy
```

This checklist provides a structured way to measure readiness and continuous improvement.

---

## Career Identity

```
☐ I have a clear professional title

☐ I understand the type of roles I am targeting

☐ My online profiles communicate the same career direction
```

