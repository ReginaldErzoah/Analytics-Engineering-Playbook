# Technical Blogging Strategy For Analytics Engineers

## Overview

Technical writing is one of the most effective ways to demonstrate expertise.

A strong technical blog shows that you can:

- Understand complex concepts
- Explain technical ideas clearly
- Document engineering decisions
- Share knowledge with others

For analytics engineers, writing is not just marketing.

It is part of professional engineering.

# Blog Article Structure

A professional technical article follows:

```
Title

↓

Introduction

↓

Problem

↓

Concept Explanation

↓

Implementation

↓

Examples

↓

Lessons Learned

↓

Conclusion
```

---

# Writing A Strong Introduction

The introduction should explain:

```
What problem exists?

Why does it matter?

What will the reader learn?
```

---

## Weak Introduction

"Today I will talk about dbt."

---

## Strong Introduction

"Many analytics teams struggle with inconsistent business metrics. This article explains how dbt helps create reliable transformation workflows and trusted analytical datasets."

---

# Explain Concepts Through Business Examples

Avoid explaining only theory.

---

## Weak

"A fact table contains numerical data."

---

## Strong

"A sales fact table stores measurable business events such as transactions, revenue, and quantity sold. This allows businesses to analyze performance over time."

---

# Include Code Examples

Good technical writing includes:

- SQL snippets
- Python examples
- Architecture diagrams
- Data models

---

# Example

```sql
SELECT
    customer_id,
    SUM(order_value) AS total_revenue
FROM orders
GROUP BY customer_id;
```

Explain:

- What the query does
- Why it matters
- How it is used

---
