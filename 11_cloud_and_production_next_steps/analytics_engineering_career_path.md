# Analytics Engineering Career Path

## Overview

Analytics engineering is a rapidly growing discipline that sits between:

- Data analysis
- Business intelligence
- Data engineering
- Software engineering

An analytics engineer builds reliable data systems that allow organizations to make better decisions.

The role focuses on:

- Transforming raw data into trusted datasets
- Building analytical models
- Creating reusable metrics
- Ensuring data quality
- Supporting business intelligence teams

---

# The Analytics Engineering Role

A traditional data workflow:

```

Data Engineer

↓

Moves Data

↓

Analyst

↓

Creates Reports

```

---

Modern analytics engineering:

```

Data Sources

↓

Analytics Engineer

↓

Reliable Data Models

↓

Analysts + BI Teams

↓

Business Decisions

```
