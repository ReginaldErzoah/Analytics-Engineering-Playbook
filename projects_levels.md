## My one recommendation

Keep **SupportOps Intelligence** as the thread that runs through your entire journey.

It can evolve like this:

* **Level 1:** Dashboard with KPIs and reports.
* **Level 2:** dbt models, dimensional warehouse, and data quality tests.
* **Level 3:** Automated ingestion from a support API with scheduled ETL.
* **Level 4:** Cloud deployment, Docker, CI/CD, monitoring, and alerts.
* **Level 5:** Forecast ticket volumes, predict SLA breaches, and estimate staffing needs with machine learning.
* **Level 6:** AI-powered support intelligence platform with anomaly detection, natural language insights, and executive decision support.

One project evolving over time demonstrates depth, long-term thinking, and engineering maturity much more effectively than starting a completely new project every time you learn a new technology.
